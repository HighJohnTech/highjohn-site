What's actually happening

Most businesses aren't losing to competitors. They're losing to voicemail.

A ringing phone that goes unanswered, a generic voicemail greeting, a lead who called three other places while waiting for a callback — none of that shows up as "lost revenue" on a report, but it is exactly that. The business isn't short on demand. It's short on response time.

What gets built

A front desk that never misses a call.

Voice answering & qualification

  • Every call answered immediately, with real qualification questions — not a generic phone tree.

Automated appointment booking

  • Qualified callers get booked directly onto the calendar — no back-and-forth, no double-booking.

CRM logging & follow-up

  • Every call, every answer, every outcome logged automatically — so nothing depends on someone remembering to write it down.

Internal alerts & handoff

  • High-value or urgent calls route to a real person immediately — the AI handles volume, not judgment calls.
