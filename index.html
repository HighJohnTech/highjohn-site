The High John Assurance Loop

Diagnose → Architect → Build → Attack → Secure → Operate → Prove.

We define the outcome, engineer the system, pressure-test agreed boundaries, harden what matters, operate responsibly, and produce evidence before expanding.

01

Diagnose

Understand the business problem, current process, tools, data, risk, and measurable opportunity.

02

Architect

Design the connected system with clear ownership, trust boundaries, permissions, fallbacks, and success measures.

03

Build

Implement the appropriate AI, automation, software, integrations, CRM, and infrastructure.

04

Attack

Test expected and unexpected behavior under authorized, agreed conditions instead of relying only on the happy path.

05

Secure

Reduce unnecessary access, fix weaknesses, strengthen controls, and document the boundaries.

06

Operate

Monitor reliability, integrations, permissions, failures, handoffs, and changing dependencies after launch.

07

Prove

Measure, document, retest, and turn what we learned into evidence and the next system improvement.
