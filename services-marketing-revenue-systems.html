Supporting build capability

Revenue & growth infrastructure when demand is leaking through the system.

HJT builds the infrastructure underneath demand: funnels, forms, attribution, conversion tracking, lead routing, booking, lifecycle automation, and the handoffs that move a prospect into the operation. This supports HJT's systems work; it is not positioned as a standalone full-service marketing agency.

What's actually happening

Demand can be real while the revenue system is still broken.

A campaign can create qualified traffic and still fail commercially because the form does not route correctly, the CRM receives incomplete data, ownership is unclear, attribution disappears, booking is difficult, or follow-up depends on memory. HJT examines the path between attention and revenue instead of assuming the ad itself is the only variable.

What gets built

Attention → capture → ownership → follow-up → conversion → evidence.

Funnels & conversion paths

  • Landing pages, forms, booking flows, and qualification paths designed around the next operational action, not just the click.

Lead capture & routing

  • Connect inbound demand to the right record, owner, state, and follow-up path with validation and failure handling considered.

Lifecycle automation

  • Use deterministic automation and AI where appropriate to move prospects through the journey while keeping human control where judgment matters.

Attribution & measurement

  • Instrument the path so leadership can distinguish traffic, qualified demand, booked opportunities, conversion, and downstream revenue outcomes.
Where it fits

The revenue layer belongs inside the operating architecture.

HJT may build revenue infrastructure as part of an automation, CRM, AI, website, systems-assessment, or broader architecture engagement. Campaign strategy or ad management can be scoped when appropriate, but HJT's primary value is making sure the system behind demand can capture, route, measure, and act on it reliably.

Before you book

What people actually ask.

Does HJT run ads?

Campaign work can be scoped when it supports the broader system, but HJT is not positioning itself as a generic media-buying agency. The core value is the connected revenue infrastructure underneath acquisition and conversion.

That can work well. HJT can focus on the systems layer — capture, routing, CRM, automation, attribution, and handoffs — while the existing agency continues to manage creative or media.

How does this connect to Automation & CRM?

Directly. Revenue infrastructure is one application of HJT's broader Automation, CRM & Integrations work.
