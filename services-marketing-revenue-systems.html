What's actually happening

Most marketing spend leaks through a broken system, not bad ads.

A campaign can drive real traffic and still produce almost nothing — because the landing page doesn't capture the lead correctly, the CRM never gets the data, or nobody follows up in time. The creative gets blamed. The system underneath it is usually the actual problem.

What gets built

Traffic → capture → CRM → follow-up → conversion.

Funnels & landing pages

  • Built to actually capture and qualify a lead — not just look good in a screenshot.

Lead capture & routing

  • Every lead from every channel lands in one place, correctly tagged, with nothing falling through the cracks.

Customer journey infrastructure

  • The connected path from first touch to customer — automated where it should be, human where it needs to be.

Campaign infrastructure & measurement

  • Tracking that actually tells you what's working, so budget goes toward what's converting, not just what's running.
