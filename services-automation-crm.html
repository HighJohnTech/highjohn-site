What's actually happening

The leak usually isn't lead volume. It's follow-up.

Most businesses generate more leads than they think — the real loss happens after the lead comes in. No second follow-up. No reminder before the appointment. No visibility into which leads went cold and why. A CRM without automation is just a more expensive spreadsheet.

What gets built

A pipeline that follows up on its own.

Lead capture & routing

  • Every lead — from the website, a call, a form, an ad — lands in one place automatically, routed to the right person or process.

Automated follow-up

  • SMS and email sequences that fire on their own — no lead goes cold because someone forgot to follow up.

Pipeline & notifications

  • Deals move through defined stages with the right people notified at the right moment — not discovered three weeks later.

Reporting & visibility

  • A real read on what's actually happening in the pipeline — not a gut feeling about how sales are going.
How it's built

One connected system, not five disconnected tools.

GoHighLevel is the primary CRM, workflow, and communication layer. We bring in n8n or Make only where a real integration gap exists — never as a default, and never just to add complexity. The goal is a system you can actually understand and run, not a black box.
