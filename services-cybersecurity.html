The problem

AI and automation can make a bad permission decision move faster.

Shared credentials, broad API access, agents with tools they do not need, secrets sitting in the wrong place, and workflows with no safe fallback can all work perfectly — until the wrong condition appears. Security-led architecture asks those questions before the incident.

Scope matters

Architecture review and active red-team testing are not the same service.

HJT can review technology risk and secure architecture as part of normal consulting and build work. Active testing of AI or technical systems requires written authorization, defined scope, clear test boundaries, and agreed stop conditions.

What gets addressed

Reduce unnecessary access. Design for failure. Keep evidence.

Identity & least privilege

  • People, services, and agents receive only the access required for the job they are expected to perform.

Secrets & data boundaries

  • Credentials, tokens, sensitive data, and internal knowledge are handled with explicit boundaries instead of convenience-driven exposure.

AI-agent permissions

  • Tool access, approval gates, human handoffs, and agent actions are scoped so an AI system cannot casually reach everything around it.

Secure integrations & recovery

  • APIs, webhooks, automation, logging, alerts, retries, and fallback behavior are treated as part of the security and reliability design.

Before you book

Clear claims. Clear boundaries.

Is every security engagement a penetration test?

No. Secure architecture, permissions review, technology-risk identification, and hardening can happen without penetration testing. Active testing is separately authorized and scoped.

Do you test third-party systems without permission?

No. HJT does not perform active testing against a third party without written authorization and defined scope.

Can security be part of an AI, automation, or software build?

Yes. That is the default HJT posture: security, permissions, data handling, and failure behavior are considered during architecture instead of waiting until after launch.
