What's actually happening

Most security problems get built in early, then discovered late.

Automation and AI systems get rushed into production with access controls as an afterthought — a shared login here, an over-permissioned integration there. None of it looks like a problem until it is one. The fix is architecture, not a scramble after something goes wrong.

What gets built

Security designed in, not patched on.

Secure system architecture

  • Systems designed with access boundaries and failure modes considered from the first diagram, not the last.

Access controls & permissions

  • Identity and access set up so the right people and systems can do exactly what they need — and nothing more.

Secure implementation

  • Automation, integrations, and AI systems built with credentials, data handling, and access reviewed as part of the build — not after launch.

Technology risk identification

  • A practical read on where the real exposure sits in your current stack, prioritized by what actually matters.
