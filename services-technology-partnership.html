After deployment

Ongoing Engineering when the system needs a standing owner.

This is not a fifth core service. It is the engagement model HJT can use after an assessment or implementation when the business needs continuing technical ownership, monitoring, maintenance, hardening, and improvement.

What's actually happening

A finished build still changes after launch.

New tools get added. APIs change. Permissions drift. Teams create workarounds. AI behavior changes as prompts, models, data, and connected tools evolve. A system that was healthy at launch can quietly become fragile if nobody owns the full operating picture.

What ongoing engineering covers

Operate, observe, improve, and harden.

System monitoring & maintenance

  • Review automation, integrations, agent behavior, failures, and operational signals so small issues do not become structural problems.

Security & permission review

  • Revisit identity, access, secrets, vendor connections, agent permissions, and data boundaries as the environment changes.

Reliability improvement

  • Fix weak retries, silent failures, brittle dependencies, bad handoffs, and recovery paths using evidence from real operation.

Technical strategy

  • Help leadership decide what to add, replace, integrate, secure, or leave alone as business needs evolve.
How it's positioned

A continuation of responsibility, not another product on the menu.

Ongoing Engineering typically follows a substantial HJT assessment, implementation, remediation, or authorized Red Team engagement. Scope is based on the systems, ownership, risk, response expectations, and improvement work the organization actually needs.

Before you book

What people actually ask.

Do I need to have already worked with HJT?

No, but HJT needs enough understanding of the current environment before taking ongoing responsibility. That may begin with a Systems Architecture Assessment or another scoped discovery engagement.

How is this different from a one-time project?

A project has a defined completion point. Ongoing Engineering exists when the business needs recurring technical ownership, monitoring, improvement, and decision support after that point.

Is this managed IT?

Not by default. HJT's focus is the architecture and systems we are responsible for: AI, automation, integrations, secure infrastructure, custom software, revenue systems, and the controls around them. Scope is defined before the engagement begins.
